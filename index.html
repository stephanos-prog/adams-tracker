Was Steven Adams right?

Adams predicted that performance across the league would slide once GTA VI comes out on Nov 19, stay down for the rest of November, and recover in December. This page follows the whole league, night by night.

    What Adams predicted

    Nov 19
    GTA VI unlocks, and performance across the league starts to slide.
    Nov 30
    The slump stays confined to November.
    December
    Players recover, and so do coaching staffs, which he expects to struggle too.

    How the verdict works

    The chart is for following along. The answer comes from an analysis registered before the season started and run once, in January 2027.

    Yes
    A clear drop after the launch, followed by a rebound.
    Half right
    The drop, without the rebound.
    No plummet
    Any drop after the launch is smaller than the registered threshold for a plummet.
    Opposite
    Players get better instead.
    Can't tell
    The data can't separate these.
